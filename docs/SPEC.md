DANSK PÅ ARBEJDE — Website Book Specification

1. Product Overview

Product name: DANSK PÅ ARBEJDE
Subtitle: Practical Danish for work and communication with colleagues
Creator: Anna Melnykova

DANSK PÅ ARBEJDE is a private, paid-access interactive website-book for Ukrainian-speaking people who are learning Danish and need practical Danish for real workplace situations.

The website itself is the product. It is not a public sales page and it is not a downloadable PDF product.

The desired emotional outcome is that the buyer:

● feels less afraid of workplace situations and conversations in Danish;
● knows what they can say in common situations;
● feels they have made a real step forward;
● feels they have finally “taken Danish into their own hands”;
● has a practical reference they can return to whenever they need it.

2. Product Principles

The website should be:

● warm
● calm
● personal
● practical
● adult-friendly
● visually similar to a high-quality digital textbook
● lightly interactive
● equally usable on mobile and desktop
● free of AI functionality
● designed without paid AI infrastructure

It should NOT feel childish, overly gamified, corporate, dark, or like a Duolingo-style app.

3. Target Audience

Ukrainian-speaking people in Denmark who are learning Danish and need Danish for work.

It should work for:

● beginners;
● people who know some Danish but struggle to speak;
● people who understand Danish but need ready-made workplace phrases;
● people who become nervous when speaking with colleagues or managers.

All explanations and interface text should use simple, natural Ukrainian, as if Anna were explaining something personally in Messenger.

4. Content Structure

The website contains 12 categories:

1.  Sygdom & fravær
2.  Arbejdstid & vagtplan
3.  Opgaver & instruktioner
4.  Møder & samtaler
5.  Samtale med chefen
6.  Kollegaskab & small talk
7.  Fejl, problemer & konflikter
8.  Telefon, beskeder & e-mail
9.  Løn, ferie & vilkår
10. Chef, HR & arbejdsplads
11. Ny på arbejdspladsen
12. Dansk på dansk

Target approximately 350–400 useful phrases and expressions, without artificially padding the content.

Every Danish phrase must appear first, followed immediately by Ukrainian.

Example:

🇩🇰 Jeg er syg i dag.
🇺🇦 Я сьогодні хворію.

5. Chapter Structure

Each chapter should contain:

1.  Short introduction/context
2.  Practical Danish phrases
3.  Audio
4.  Mini-dialogues
5.  “Від перекладача” personal explanation
6.  Typical mistakes
7.  Ready-made messages
8.  Small practice section
9.  Vocabulary / mini-dictionary

These elements belong inside the relevant chapter.

6. Phrase Presentation

The experience should feel like a textbook rather than a card-heavy app.

Example:

🇩🇰 Jeg er syg i dag.

🇺🇦 Я сьогодні хворію.

🔊 Audio

❤️ Зберегти

Complex or especially useful phrases may have expandable explanations.

Danish and Ukrainian should be visually distinct, but Ukrainian must not look secondary.

7. Audio

● Danish pronunciation only
● website playback only
● unlimited replay
● audio cannot be downloaded
● use a simple native/browser audio player

8. Saved Phrases

Each phrase can have:

❤️ Зберегти

Saved phrases belong to the user’s account.

There should be a dedicated page:

❤️ Мої збережені фрази

9. Explanations

Complex or important phrases may have an expandable:

Пояснення

The normal phrase view should remain clean.

10. “Від перекладача”

Each chapter must contain at least one personal “Від перекладача” block.

Style:

● cream/pink visual block;
● warm;
● personal;
● simple conversational Ukrainian;
● identified as Anna where appropriate.

Additional blocks can appear when genuinely useful.

11. Typical Mistakes

Use:

❌ Incorrect / unnatural phrase
☑ Better Danish phrase
💡 Why?

Explain in simple Ukrainian.

12. Mini-Dialogues

Mini-dialogues should show Danish first.

Default state:

● Danish dialogue visible;
● Ukrainian translation hidden.

Button:

Показати переклад

Audio should be available where appropriate.

13. Ready-Made Messages
 Each chapter should include relevant practical ready-made messages for:

● managers;
● colleagues;
● HR;
● workplace contacts.

Danish first, Ukrainian immediately underneath.

14. Practice

There must be NO AI-powered exercises.

Exercises are static/pre-written.

Preferred format:

Що сказати начальнику, якщо ти захворіла?

○ Jeg har ferie.
○ Jeg er syg.
○ Jeg kommer tidligere.

Immediate feedback:

☑ Правильно!

or

❌ Спробуйте ще раз.

Approximately 3–5 exercises per chapter.

15. Search

Search is a major feature.

It must work with Ukrainian and Danish keywords.

Example:

User searches я захворіла → relevant Danish phrases.

User searches chef → relevant manager/workplace phrases.

The search page should include quick popular searches:

🔍 Що вам потрібно сказати?

я захворіла шеф перерва не зрозуміла відпустка

If an exact phrase is not found:

● tell the user;
● show similar relevant phrases/situations.

Do not use AI semantic search. Use a prepared keyword/index structure.

16. Homepage After Login

First screen:

> 💛 Ви вже зробили перший крок.
>
> Цей матеріал створений для того, щоб вам було легше говорити данською на роботі — без страху, що ви скажете щось неправильно.

Then show:

● the 12 category cards;
● large search;
● saved phrases.

17. Category Cards

Minimalistic cards with:

● Danish category name;
● short Ukrainian description;
● phrase count.

18. Navigation

Support both:

● sequential chapter learning;
● quick reference/search.

19. Progress

Light, pressure-free progress tracking.

Show:

● overall progress;
● checkmarks next to completed/visited chapters.

Example:

📚 4 / 12 kapitler

✓ Sygdom & fravær

20. Final Completion Screen

After all 12 categories:

> 🌸 Du gjorde det!
>
> Ви пройшли весь матеріал.
>
> Тепер, коли вам потрібно щось сказати на роботі, ви знаєте, де це знайти.
>
> Датська вже не просто “десь там”. Ви взяли її у свої руки.
>
> — Anna Melnykova

Keep it calm and encouraging.

21. Visual Design

Direction: modern educational textbook + personal brand

Palette:

● warm cream;
● soft pink;
● light blue;
● warm light accents.

Avoid dark navy/corporate blue.

The visual experience should be cozy, calm and professional.

22. Typography

Use:

● a clean, modern readable font for main text;
● a subtle handwritten-style font for selected headings/accent elements.

Use handwritten styling sparingly.

23. Branding

Do NOT use Anna’s photograph.

Use:

Created by Anna Melnykova

throughout the product in appropriate places.

The product should feel like an independent professional brand while retaining Anna’s personal voice.

24. Animations

Almost no animation.

Keep the experience calm and textbook-like.

Only subtle functional transitions where useful.

25. Account & Access

The website is private and paid-access only.

Customers purchase access manually outside the website, primarily through Facebook/Messenger.

Registration

Customer registers with:

● phone number;
● password.

The phone number must already be authorized by Anna.

If authorized → registration/access proceeds.
If not authorized → access is denied.

26. Admin Panel

Private admin area for Anna.

Anna can:

● add/authorize phone numbers;
● remove/block users;
● view all users;
● see last login;
● manually terminate a user’s active session;
● view basic usage statistics;
● open a user’s profile;
● initiate password reset.

The admin panel should be simple enough for Anna to use without technical knowledge.

27. One Active Session

Only one active session per customer is allowed.

If the same user logs in on another device:

1.  Previous session is invalidated.
2.  New device becomes the active session.
3.  Previous device is logged out.
4.  User receives a clear warning that the previous session was ended.

This is intended to reduce account sharing while allowing normal use across devices.

28. Password Reset

Password recovery is handled manually by Anna.

Flow:

1.  Anna opens the user’s profile in the admin panel.
2.  Anna selects Reset/Forgot Password.
3. System generates a verification/reset code.
4.  Anna sends the code to the customer manually.
5.  Customer uses it to set a new password.

6.  Access Duration

Access remains active until Anna closes, revokes, or changes it.

No automatic one-year expiration is required.

30. NO PDF / NO DOWNLOAD FUNCTIONALITY

PDF export/download functionality has been completely removed.

There must be:

● NO Save as PDF button;
● NO PDF export;
● NO downloadable PDF;
● NO printable PDF;
● NO chapter PDF downloads;
● NO page PDF downloads.

The website itself is the product.

Audio also remains website-only and cannot be downloaded.

Created by Anna Melnykova remains a creator/brand mark throughout the website.

31. NO AI

There must be:

● NO AI chatbot;
● NO AI tutor;
● NO AI exercise checking;
● NO AI semantic search;
● NO AI API;
● NO paid AI infrastructure.

All content and exercises are prepared in advance.

32. Interface Language

All website interface controls are in Ukrainian.

Examples:

● Пошук
● Зберегти
● Мої збережені фрази
● Показати переклад
● Пояснення
● Перевірити
● Правильно!
● Спробуйте ще раз

Danish is used for the learning material.

33. Responsive Design

Work equally well on:

● iPhone/mobile browsers;
● tablets;
● laptops;
● desktop.

Mobile must not be an afterthought.

34. Existing Content Base

The current content base contains the 12 chapters above plus:

● ready-made messages;
● translator explanations;
● typical mistakes;
● “what to say if…” situations;
● mini-dictionary/vocabulary;
● mini-dialogues.

These should be integrated into the relevant chapters.

35. Content Writing Rules

Danish first.
Ukrainian immediately underneath.

Ukrainian should be:

● natural;
● simple;
● conversational;
● supportive.

Avoid robotic, overly formal or unnecessarily long explanations.

36. Product Philosophy

Do not sell the product primarily as “350–400 phrases”.

The deeper value is:

> “I know what to say.”

> “I am less afraid of workplace conversations.”

> “I finally took Danish into my own hands.”

The website should make the user feel they have taken a meaningful step toward better communication and better opportunities in Denmark.

37. Main User Journey

1.  Customer purchases access outside the website.
1.  Anna authorizes their phone number in the admin panel.
1.  Customer registers with phone number + password.
1.  Customer sees the warm welcome screen.
1.  Customer browses categories or searches.
1.  Customer reads Danish + Ukrainian, listens to audio, opens explanations and dialogues, saves phrases and completes exercises.
1.  Customer returns whenever a real workplace situation appears.
1.  After completing all categories, the final completion screen appears.

1.  Non-Goals

The website is NOT:

● an AI language tutor;
● a social platform;
● a public blog;
● a marketplace;
● a public sales landing page;
● a downloadable PDF product;
● an audio download library;
● a gamified language app;
● a complex LMS requiring heavy administration.

39. Technical Priorities

Priority order:

1.  Private paid-content protection
2.  Reliable authentication
3.  One active session per account
4.  Simple admin management
5.  Excellent mobile + desktop reading experience
6.  Fast search
7.  Reliable audio playback
8.  Saved phrases
9.  Static exercises
10. Lightweight progress tracking
11. Calm textbook-style visual design
12. Low/no recurring AI-related costs

13. Final Product Definition

DANSK PÅ ARBEJDE is a private interactive digital book created by Anna Melnykova for Ukrainian-speaking learners of Danish.

It combines:

● practical workplace Danish;
● Danish + Ukrainian explanations;
● audio pronunciation;
● realistic dialogues;
● personal translator notes;
● typical mistakes;
● ready-made messages;
● static practice;
● search;
● saved phrases;
● light progress tracking.

It should feel like a warm, high-quality digital textbook that the customer can return to whenever a real workplace situation appears.

The website itself is the product.
There is NO PDF download/export functionality whatsoever.

There is NO AI functionality whatsoever.

Customers receive private account access after purchase, with only one active session per customer at a time.

Created by Anna Melnykova.
